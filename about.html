ABOUT THE PROJECT

CyberTrace is a student project about safer decisions.

We built a small, interactive investigation to make cybersecurity awareness easier to practise.

PROJECT NOTESCYBERTRACE / 2026
WHY WE BUILT IT

Security is easier to learn when you can try it.

In a world where every click matters, curiosity and critical thinking help us make safer digital decisions.

THE IDEA

Make safety something you practise.

CyberTrace puts the visitor in the role of a digital investigator. You read a suspicious message, inspect its details, and make a decision based on evidence.

We wanted the lesson to feel like a task, not a textbook.

THE PROBLEM

Online threats rarely announce themselves.

Suspicious emails, misleading links, and requests for personal information are part of everyday internet use.

CyberTrace gives learners a safe place to slow down, notice warning signs, and understand why they matter.

WHAT YOU CAN DO HERE

Four steps from a message to a decision.
